Submit Your eBook for Advertising

Complete the form below to apply for an eBook ad placement. Here’s how it works:

  • ASIN Verification: Enter your eBook's ASIN (Amazon Standard Identification Number). We'll verify it to ensure it's eligible for promotion.
  • Sale Price & Run Date: Choose your promotional price and the date you'd like the ad to run.
  • Genre Targeting: Select your eBook’s genre so we can match it with the right audience of readers.
  • Ad Review Process: Once submitted, your ad request will go through a brief review. We’ll notify you by email once it’s approved or if any changes are needed.
  • Payment Info: You'll provide your credit card details during submission, but you won't be charged unless your ad is approved.

This form is designed to make applying for a featured ad fast and straightforward. If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to our support team: support@booktokusa.com.
